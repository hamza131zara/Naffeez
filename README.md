# Naffeez

Premium men's fashion e-commerce platform for Pakistan, with a monochrome editorial storefront, light/dark themes, a dedicated Product Studio shopping experience, admin-managed catalog and inventory, COD and manually verified Easypaisa/bank QR payments, WhatsApp support, and downloadable invoices.

**Website:** https://naffeez.com  
**Launch language:** English  
**Launch currency:** PKR  
**Initial catalog:** Casual Shirts, Dress Shirts, Seasonal Trousers, Winter Trousers and Hoodies.

AI styling, virtual try-on, face previews and advanced 3D are planned future modules. They are not required for the initial commerce MVP.

## Working principles

- Preserve approved scope and document changes.
- Inspect existing files before editing; do not overwrite working code blindly.
- Keep the initial application footprint small.
- Keep secrets and customer/payment data out of source control.
- Do not claim a feature is implemented or tested without evidence.
- Core browsing, checkout and invoices must work independently of future AI/3D services.
