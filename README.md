# Naffeez

Premium men's fashion e-commerce platform for Pakistan, with a monochrome editorial storefront, Light/Dark themes, a dedicated Product Studio shopping experience, admin-managed products and inventory, COD and manually verified Easypaisa/bank QR payments, WhatsApp support, invoices, a Journal and customer notification preferences.

**Website:** https://naffeez.com  
**Launch language:** English  
**Launch currency:** PKR  
**Initial catalog:** Casual Shirts, Dress Shirts, Seasonal Trousers, Winter Trousers and Hoodies.

AI styling, virtual try-on, face previews and advanced 3D are future modules, not MVP dependencies.

## Working principles

- Preserve approved scope and document changes.
- Inspect repository files before editing; do not overwrite working functionality blindly.
- Keep the initial application footprint small and modular.
- Keep secrets and customer/payment data out of source control.
- Marketing email requires explicit topic opt-in; account email or purchase alone is not consent.
- Core browsing, checkout and invoices must work independently of email, AI and 3D providers.
- Do not claim a feature is implemented, tested or deployed without evidence.
